FAQ

How do you play charades?

One player acts out or describes a character without saying the name while the group guesses before time runs out. In Mimic you pick a theme (movies, TV, anime or celebs), act out each card and score points per correct guess.

Is it free?

Yes. The free pack includes a sample of each theme. Extra packs are one-time purchases inside the app.

Do I need internet or an account?

No. Play as a group with a single phone, no sign-up.

Can I buy packs from this site?

No. Purchases only exist inside the Android app, with Google Play.

What does the web demo include?
